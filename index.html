ABOUT MINNIMO

Building thoughtful consumer products for modern everyday living.

Minnimo Products Pvt. Ltd. is a consumer-products company focused on discovering useful ideas, developing and sourcing practical products, refining them for quality and usability, and preparing them for today’s digital-first marketplace.

Our approach brings together product research, sourcing, quality control, branding, packaging and market readiness under one focused system. REEPSO is Minnimo’s consumer brand — created to turn these efforts into products designed for real everyday needs.
