About Minnimo

We look at ordinary routines and ask a simple question: can this everyday experience be made better?

Minnimo Products Pvt. Ltd. is a consumer products company focused on developing, sourcing and bringing thoughtfully designed everyday products to market.

Our work spans product research, vendor development, sourcing, packaging, branding, quality oversight and multi-channel distribution. Products are marketed primarily through our consumer brand REEPSO.
