Cookie Policy

This page explains the website’s approach to cookies and similar browser technologies.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files or browser-storage technologies that websites may use to remember preferences, support functionality or understand website usage.

Current Website Use

The Minnimo corporate website may use essential browser functionality and may in future use analytics or similar tools to understand aggregate website performance. Where non-essential cookie tools are introduced, this policy may be updated accordingly.

Third-Party Services

Links or embedded services from third parties may use their own cookies or technologies. Those services are governed by the relevant provider’s own policies.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Restricting certain cookies may affect some website functions.
