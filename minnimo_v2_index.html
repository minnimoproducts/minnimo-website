About Minnimo

From everyday problem to market-ready product.

Minnimo Products Pvt. Ltd. is a consumer-products company working across product research, development, sourcing, packaging, branding, quality and digital-first distribution. Our focus is on useful products with thoughtful functionality and a clear consumer purpose.

Product thinkingWe begin with genuine everyday use cases and look for practical ways to improve them.
Development & sourcingWe work with suitable product and manufacturing partners based on each product's requirements.
Brand & marketWe build the packaging, presentation and digital experience needed to take products to modern consumers.
